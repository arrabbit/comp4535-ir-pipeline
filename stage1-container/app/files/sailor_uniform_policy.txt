Sailor Guardian Uniform Policy

Each guardian must transform when acting in their Sailor Guardian capacity.

1. Uniform colours
   Each guardian wears the standard sailor outfit in their assigned colour:

   Sailor Moon        Blue and red
   Sailor Mercury     Blue
   Sailor Mars        Red
   Sailor Jupiter     Green
   Sailor Venus       Orange
   Sailor Chibi Moon  Pink
   Sailor Uranus      Navy
   Sailor Neptune     Teal
   Sailor Pluto       Maroon
   Sailor Saturn      Purple

2. Accessories
   Tiaras, brooches and gloves must be worn at all times while transformed.

3. Civilian identity
   Guardians must not reveal another guardian's civilian identity to anyone
   outside the team.

Questions about this policy can be directed to Luna.
