Crystal Tokyo Cat Feeding Schedule

Luna, Artemis and Diana are fed twice daily, at 8:00 and 18:00.
Feeding duty rotates each day of the week:

   Monday     Usagi Tsukino
   Tuesday    Ami Mizuno
   Wednesday  Rei Hino
   Thursday   Makoto Kino
   Friday     Minako Aino
   Saturday   Mamoru Chiba
   Sunday     Chibiusa Tsukino

Notes
- Diana gets a smaller portion.
- Do not feed table scraps, no matter how much Luna asks.
- If you cannot cover your day, trade with another guardian and notify Luna at
  least 24 hrs in advance.
